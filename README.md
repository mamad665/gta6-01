## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network, just static files under `game/`.

## The game

You are Marco Vale, a wheelman with a debt in Solmar City — a sun-soaked coastal town of grids, beaches, docks and one very jumpy police department. Rosa Delgado runs the garage on 5th & Marlin, and she has four jobs for you:

1. **First Run** — fetch a package from Port Docks and bring it home.
2. **Hot Wheels** — steal the yellow Comet GT by Sunset Mall and deliver it.
3. **The Drop** — run a package to the Marina while the PD is jumpy.
4. **Bank Van Blues** — stop the Solmar Bank van, grab the cash, lose the cops at Pier 9.

Systems: walk and drive anywhere in the city, traffic and pedestrians, stealing any car (the driver bails out), a 5-star wanted level with police cruisers that chase, ram and shoot, fist and pistol combat, health/ammo pickups, a HUD with cash, health, wanted stars and a live minimap, a title screen, pause, and autosave to localStorage.

Everything is original: the city, the names, the story, the art and the sounds (all synthesized with WebAudio — no audio files). Nothing from Rockstar Games or any other commercial game.

## Controls

- **WASD** move / drive · **SHIFT** run · **E** enter, steal or exit a car
- **F** punch · **CLICK** shoot the pistol · **SPACE** handbrake
- **P** pause · **M** mute · **ENTER** start / continue
